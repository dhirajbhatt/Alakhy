Products / HireLens

AI-native hiring
intelligence.

HireLens is an applicant tracking system built for teams who make technical hires. It understands the difference between a candidate who interviewed well and one who can actually do the work.

The problem

Hiring tools built
for volume.

Most ATS platforms are built for HR teams processing high volumes of applications. They rank by keyword, filter by credential, and optimise for speed. They were not built for teams where every hire matters.

01
Keyword matching fails

Filters built on job titles and buzzwords miss the candidates who solve problems and eliminate the ones who just know the vocabulary.

02
No technical depth

Generic platforms cannot assess whether someone can architect a system, debug at scale, or lead an engineering team under pressure.

03
Pipeline opacity

You cannot see where strong candidates dropped out, which assessors are reliable, or what patterns separate hires who work from ones who do not.

What HireLens does

Built for
technical hiring.

Four core capabilities. Each designed for teams where the cost of a wrong hire — or a missed one — is significant.

01
Intelligent Screening

AI that reads applications the way a senior engineer would. It looks for evidence of capability, not just credentials. Signals from what candidates have built, shipped, and solved — not what they have listed.

02
Technical Assessment

Role-specific assessments designed with technical depth. Not generic coding challenges — problem-solving scenarios calibrated to the actual demands of the role and the level of seniority required.

03
Pipeline Intelligence

Visibility into where candidates drop, which stages create friction, and which assessors make good decisions. The data to improve your process — not just your throughput.

04
Hiring Analytics

Patterns across your hiring cohorts. Which sources produce candidates who stay. Which interview signals predict performance. Institutional knowledge that compounds over time.
